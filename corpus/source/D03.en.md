# Agent upgrade guide: 3.8 to 4.2

Applies to upgrading the Sentriq Agent from 3.8 to 4.2. Read the per-component
timing table before scheduling — the values differ by component and getting them
wrong causes the upgrade to stall.

## Pre-upgrade checks

- Confirm the collector and store are healthy (`sentriqctl store health`).
- Confirm no active P1/P2 incident.
- Snapshot the current policy so it can be restored.

## Per-component upgrade timing

Upgrade in the order below. Each component has a different drain window and a
different safe concurrency — the number of nodes you may upgrade at once without
losing coverage. **Do not read across rows: each value belongs to its own
component.**

| Component | Order | Drain window | Safe concurrency | Rollback time |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| sq-policy | 1 | 2 minutes | 1 node | 30 seconds |
| sq-collector | 2 | 10 minutes | 1 of 3 nodes | 5 minutes |
| sq-store | 3 | 20 minutes | 1 of 4 nodes | 15 minutes |
| sq-detect | 4 | 5 minutes | 1 of 2 nodes | 2 minutes |
| sq-relay | 5 | 3 minutes | 1 of 2 nodes | 1 minute |
| sq-agent | 6 | n/a (rolling) | 5% of fleet | per-endpoint |

The most common upgrade mistake is applying the collector's 10-minute drain
window to the store, which needs 20. The store shard rebalance is not finished at
10 minutes, and upgrading the next node then triggers `SQ-6008`.

## Agent rollout

Agents upgrade in waves of 5% of the fleet. Wait for each wave to report healthy
on 4.2 before releasing the next. A wave that does not report within its window
is held automatically; investigate before overriding.

## Post-upgrade verification

```
sentriqctl fleet version-report
```

Confirms the version distribution across the fleet. Any endpoint stuck on 3.8
after the rollout window needs the offline runbook (D06) — but check first that it
is not simply a slow wave.

## Rollback

Each component rolls back independently within its rollback-time column above.
Roll back in reverse order (agent first, policy last). Do not roll back the store
while a shard rebalance is in progress.
